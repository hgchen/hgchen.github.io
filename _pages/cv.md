  Short bio - Huaijin Chen is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, where he directs the Computational Imaging and Robotic Perception (CIRP) Lab.
  His research focuses on imaging and perception systems enabled by physics-aware AI, with applications in intelligent sensing, robotics, and scientific imaging.
  He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Rice University in 2019 and his B.S. in Imaging Science from the Rochester Institute of Technology.
  Before joining academia, he held research and leadership positions at Vayu Robotics and SenseBrain and conducted research at NVIDIA and IBM.
  His research has received funding and support from the National Science Foundation (NSF), Google, NVIDIA, and other organizations.
  He has authored over 20 peer-reviewed publications in leading venues, including CVPR, ICCV, ICCP, and Optics Express, and holds four U.S. patents.
  He served as Local Chair for ICCV 2025 and will serve in the same role for CoRL 2029.
  He has served on the program committees of ICCP and IJCAI and regularly reviews for leading conferences and journals, including CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICLR, IEEE TPAMI, and IEEE TIP.
  His honors include third place for the Best Paper Award at the RSS 2025 Workshop on Contact Dynamics, the 2025 Farm Robotics Challenge Grand Prize, the ICCV 2021 Outstanding Reviewer Award, the ICCP 2019 Best Poster Award, and the Texas Instruments Distinguished Graduate Student Fellowship.
