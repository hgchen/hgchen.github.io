I direct the Computational Imaging and Robotic Perception (CIRP) Lab. We develop imaging and perception systems for *robotics* and *scientific instrumentation*. We design sensors and algorithms together to help robots understand their surroundings and scientists measure how physical systems change. [Explore our current research](https://hgchen.com/cirp-lab/).

Prior to UHM, from 2022 to 2024, I was the founding Computational Imaging Lead at Vayu Robotics, focusing on enhancing autonomous vehicle perception through innovative imaging techniques. Vayu is backed by [Khosla Ventures](https://www.vayurobotics.com/press-releases/vayu-robotics-emerges-from-stealth-with-12-7-million-in-seed-funding-from-khosla-ventures-and-lockheed-martin-ventures), and guided by Nobel laureate [Geoffrey Hinton](https://twitter.com/geoffreyhinton/status/1712171599636435105). From 2019 to 2022, I was with SenseBrain Technology, where I contributed to computational photography on smartphones. My team made strides in areas like novel image sensor, [under-display camera tech](https://jnjaby.github.io/projects/UDC/), [3D ToF imaging](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~felipe/project-pages/2021-itof2dtof/), and [neural image compression](https://github.com/SenseBrain/JPD-SE).

I earned my Ph.D. in electrical and computer engineering from Rice University in 2019 and my B.S. in imaging science from the Rochester Institute of Technology in 2013. At Rice, I studied how to incorporate imaging physics into deep learning. During my studies, I visited research groups at Cornell, Northwestern, and Brown and interned at NVIDIA Research, IBM, and Light Labs. I also co-founded and led [SenseWatch](https://sense.watch), a startup that used wearable devices to recognize gestures and monitor vital signs.
